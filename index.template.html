How shipping a car from NYC works

Three steps. You never drive to a terminal, and nobody calls the day before pickup to "adjust" the price.

  1. Get your price

    Two ZIP codes and your vehicle. You see the exact all-in number right away — no email chase, no callback required.

  2. Book and we dispatch

    A small deposit holds your price. We assign a carrier we've verified with FMCSA and confirm a pickup window, usually 1 to 5 days out in the NYC metro.

  3. Inspect, load, deliver

    The driver inspects the car with you at pickup and again at delivery. You pay the balance to the driver when the car is in front of you — not before.

Questions people ask before they book

How much does it cost to ship a car from New York City?

Shipping a car from NYC typically runs $500–$900 for East Coast routes and $1,200–$1,800 coast to coast on an open carrier. Price depends on distance, vehicle size, trailer type, season, and whether the vehicle runs. The quote tool at the top of the page gives you an exact all-in price in about 60 seconds.

How long does car shipping from New York take?

Most pickups happen within 1–5 days of booking. Transit is roughly 1–3 days to Florida or the Southeast, 3–5 days to Texas and the Midwest, and 7–10 days to California and the West Coast.

Is my car insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier in our network carries active cargo insurance that covers your vehicle from pickup to delivery, and we verify each carrier's FMCSA authority and insurance before dispatch. You pay no deductible for carrier-caused damage.

Should I choose open or enclosed transport?

Open transport is the most popular and affordable option and is safe for everyday vehicles. Enclosed transport costs about 30–50% more and protects luxury, exotic, classic, or freshly restored vehicles from weather and road debris.

Can you pick up my car in Manhattan, Brooklyn, or Queens?

Yes. We ship from all five boroughs, Long Island, Westchester, and Northern New Jersey. Where a full-size car hauler cannot safely access a street, your driver will arrange a nearby meeting point such as a large parking lot.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No payment is required to get a quote. A small deposit is collected once a carrier is assigned, and the balance is paid to the driver on delivery. There are no hidden fees — the price you are quoted is the price you pay.
